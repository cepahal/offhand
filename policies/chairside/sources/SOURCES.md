**Cite as:** U.S. Office of Personnel Management. *Aetna Dental: A Nationwide Dental PPO Plan, 2026* (FEDVIP plan brochure 02AP-01). Washington, DC: OPM; 2025. https://www.opm.gov/healthcare-insurance/healthcare/plan-information/plans/pdf/2026/brochures/02AP-01.pdf (accessed 2026-11-10).

**Why we may use it:** OPM publishes it openly as the official statement of benefits for a federal program, so it can be read and cited without restriction. The brochure carries no copyright notice except for CDT procedure codes and descriptors, which are © American Dental Association. Quote individual CDT codes only as needed, and don't republish the code tables.

**What it gives the rulebook:**
- Frequency limits, for example adult and child cleanings (D1110, D1120) limited to twice per calendar year, and sealants once per tooth in a 3-year period.
- Annual benefit maximums for Class A, B, and C services, separately for High Option and Standard Option and for in-network and out-of-network care, plus an orthodontic (Class D) lifetime maximum.
- Age limits, for example sealants, caries medicaments, and space maintainers limited to children under 19. No age limit on orthodontics was found in this brochure.
- Waiting periods: the brochure states explicitly that there is **no** waiting period on either option. That's useful for false test sentences.

**Caveat:** This is one specific real plan, not "dental insurance in general." Patient-facing text should say "under this example plan."
