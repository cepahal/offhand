**Why we may use it:** The consortium's English assessment-instruments package says that the Diagnostic Criteria Table and Decision Trees "were created for the Consortium website and community, and reside in the public domain." Its other instruments are marked "No permission required to reproduce, translate, display, or distribute." That statement is in the package at https://inform-iadr.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/DC-TMD-English-Assessment-Instruments_2020_02_06_secured.pdf (checked 2026-11-10).

**What it gives the rulebook:** The history and examination criteria for each diagnosis. For disc displacement, it covers four subtypes: with reduction; with reduction and intermittent locking; without reduction, with limited opening; and without reduction, without limited opening. The limited-opening cutoff is maximum assisted opening under 40 mm, including vertical incisal overlap. It also gives the sensitivity and specificity of each diagnosis.

**Caveat:** The clinical criteria detect disc displacement with reduction poorly (sensitivity 0.34, specificity 0.92 per the table). The table itself doesn't discuss imaging. When the patient's MRI is the scan being shown, rules should defer to the MRI-confirmed finding and treat a DC/TMD clinical diagnosis as separate.
