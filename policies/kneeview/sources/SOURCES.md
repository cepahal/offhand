Writing our own rules that paraphrase its criteria, with a citation, is fine. Don't present an edited copy of the paper as the original.

**What it gives the rulebook:** Consensus statements on return to sport after ACL injury and reconstruction. These cover time since surgery, objective criteria (strength and hop-test symmetry), psychological readiness, and the continuum from return to participation, to return to sport, to return to performance.

**Caveat:** These are expert-consensus statements, not a single pass/fail test. Rules should say "the consensus recommends…" rather than "you are cleared when…".
